## 2. Question, origin, horizon and population

**Question:** At an offseason decision cutoff, do the frozen rankings select assets with more subsequent MLB reference production, using only information legally available at that cutoff?

- Design cutoff for the intended launch: `2026-10-01T00:00:00-04:00` (`2026-10-01T04:00:00Z`). This is a proposed information cutoff, not an adopted launch date. A later publication must retain this cutoff or explicitly version it before inputs/outcomes are inspected.
- Historical origin `T_y` means **October 1 of origin year y**, not January 1 of first target year. MLB target seasons are `y+1, y+2, y+3`; launch target is **2027–2029**.
- **New prospect-ordering design choice:** next **five complete MLB regular seasons**, `y+1` through `y+5`, unweighted cumulative reference production; launch context is **2027–2031**. Five years allows arrival delay to appear in realized production without inventing an arrival model. It does not change the application's three-year value horizon or automatically adopt this longer horizon as a public product promise.
- Grain/key: one `(MLBAM asset, origin timestamp, evaluation claim, league-contract version)`; retain role-season components, collapse them to one asset before metrics. MLB/prospect eligibility is frozen at origin under the exact admitted cumulative service/graduation rules. No selection by future debut, survival, future playing time or completed-label success.
- Include the full origin-eligible universe, including cold starts, non-arrivals, later graduates and later role changes. MLB candidate minimum playing-time filters belong in a disclosed coverage ledger, not an undisclosed redefinition of the population. SP/RP/hitter slices use origin roles; a two-way slice remains one asset.
- A prospect that never reaches MLB has known zero MLB production only when the complete outcome window and identity coverage establish it. Injury, retirement and non-arrival remain outcomes. Unresolved identity, missing seasons and censored windows are missing/immature, not zero.

As of this document's date, **no complete 2026 regular-season label is final**. October availability of final-2026 data must be proved at the proposed cutoff; the calendar date alone does not prove a source has published a final version.

## 3. Reference target: precise research units, limited claim

### 3.1 Reference league and annual target

Use source-default **5x5 categories**, unit category weights: hitters R/HR/RBI/SB/AVG; pitchers W/SV/K/ERA/WHIP. Source: `web/category_registry.py`, `web/config_builder.py`, and `src/league_values/engine.py` in the bound season candidate. This selects a research reference, not qualification of every actual league preset.

For each origin, freeze reference constants from the **three most recent complete MLB regular seasons whose final source versions were available before T**. Every player-role-season with recorded positive PA or IP enters that role's reference population once; do not impose a future-outcome filter. Use a pooled player-season population, not equal player weights across careers. Identity, counting-stat completeness, innings conversion and reference inclusion must be bound before calculating constants. If three complete legal seasons are absent, the fold is ineligible; do not substitute current revisions.

Calculate the source engine's category impacts with explicit fixed ratio baselines: aggregate H/AB for AVG, 9×ER/IP for ERA, and (BB+H_ALLOWED)/IP for WHIP from that reference population. Freeze each category's population standard deviation of impacts, using the engine's population-variance convention. Keep its ratio denominator rules: AVG impact is zero at AB <= 30; ERA/WHIP impact is zero at IP <= 10. These are retained engine semantics, not claims that low-volume ratio production has no real effect. Missing numerators/denominators must be rejected before the engine's permissive defaults can turn them into zero. A zero/nonfinite reference scale blocks the fold.

Define annual **reference category production above zero production**:

`U(asset, season | T) = sum_role sum_applicable_category [impact(actual role-season; fixed T ratio baseline) / fixed T category SD]`.

Equivalently, this is the fixed-reference category engine value minus the same-role zero-production engine value. Centering constants therefore cancel; a verified no-production role contributes zero, and negative ratio contributions remain negative. Two-way actual production includes each role once. No 0–100 scaling, role anchor, graduation floor, age/volume multiplier, clipping, survival factor or reliability discount belongs in the realized target.

This target is an explicitly chosen evaluation yardstick built from existing category impacts, not an adopted model or an empirically qualified cross-role market-value scale. It credits aggregate season production without lineup optimization. **Replacement for this ranking target is zero production**, not the best waiver player or an occupied roster slot. A trade/replacement target is still a different unresolved estimand.

### 3.2 Horizon targets and coverage

- R-MLB and any future U evaluation: `H3 = (U[y+1] + 0.72 U[y+2] + 0.48 U[y+3]) / 2.20`. The weights and weighted-mean convention are retained from source, not optimized here.
- R-P: `P5 = sum(U[y+1] ... U[y+5])`. Non-arrival years contribute known zeros when verified. Later production after year five is outside this target, not a failure to value all career upside.
- Retain annual/category/role contributions. Unweighted three-year mean is a predeclared sensitivity diagnostic, never an alternative promotion metric chosen after results.
- Primary preset coverage is **5x5 only**. The source also exposes `obp`, `6x6`, `sv_hld`, `7x7`, `7x7_ops`, and `points`; each needs its own bound target, population and claim-specific evidence before asserting qualification. Roto's adjacent-impact-gap output is not observed standings utility. Custom configurations inherit no empirical guarantee from the reference result.

## 6. Metrics, coverage, uncertainty and proposed pass rules

### Primary ranking measure

Spearman correlation between candidate score and realized H3 (R-MLB) or P5 (R-P), **higher is better**, on identical admitted asset-origin rows for every candidate and comparator. Use average ranks for tied scores and tied outcomes, including non-arrival zeros. Undefined correlation, absent comparator or zero target variance makes a fold non-evaluable, not a zero-scored win. Primary development aggregate is the unweighted mean of the three origin correlations, a summary over potentially overlapping dependent windows rather than independent replications; report each fold and paired baseline gap.

No outcome-driven complete-case population selection. First freeze the origin universe and missing-input ledger without labels; then account for label coverage. Publish full-universe coverage, matched metric population and reasons for every exclusion. Do not silently remove the same difficult players from every comparator to manufacture apparent parity.

**Proposed admission floors, not power guarantees:** at least 200 unique matched assets per claim/origin; at least 95% of the frozen origin universe has complete candidate/comparator inputs and mature labels; at least 90% coverage within each origin role and cold-start/graduate slice. The metric population must have unique reconciled MLBAM identities and 100% required-field provenance/completeness. Any selected top-50 candidate asset with missing outcome/source/comparator coverage blocks the top-group claim. A slice with fewer than 30 matched assets is descriptive and explicitly unqualified as a separate claim. Threshold failure means hold/re-charter prospectively; it never permits hidden exclusions.

**Proposed superiority rule:** mean development Spearman improvement at least **0.02** against every required informative comparator, positive in at least two of three development origins, no origin gap below **-0.05**. Confirmation must improve at least **0.02** against each comparator and its paired uncertainty lower bound must exceed zero. These margins express a deliberately modest practical bar chosen before outcomes; feasibility and power are unknown. Null context alone cannot confer a pass.

**Additional absolute-direction condition for useful-ordering qualification:** candidate mean development Spearman must be **strictly positive**, and candidate confirmation Spearman must be **strictly positive with a one-sided 95% player-cluster lower bound strictly above zero**. Use the same confirmation resamples as the paired comparator gaps below. Both absolute direction and relative superiority must pass; if absolute direction fails, report any relative improvement truthfully while withholding useful-ordering qualification.

Within sufficiently sized roles, report every comparator gap and flag any gap below -0.05 for failure of that role-specific claim. A pooled pass cannot be marketed as separate hitter, SP, RP, two-way, graduate or cold-start qualification when that slice fails or is too small. Failure in a predeclared slice must appear in the release decision; no favorable-slice-only summary.

### Uncertainty and secondary diagnostics

Use 2,000 paired player-cluster bootstrap replicates, fixed seed `20261001`: sample unique MLBAM assets with replacement, carrying every selected origin/role record for that asset together, and recompute candidate correlations and paired gaps on identical resamples. For each claim, use one-sided lower percentile bounds for comparator gaps at `alpha = 0.05 / number_of_required_informative_comparators` so the comparator family is not treated as several unadjusted 95% tests. Preserve the unadjusted intervals too. For the additional confirmation absolute-direction condition, report the candidate Spearman one-sided 95% lower percentile bound (`alpha = 0.05`) from those same confirmation resamples. More than 5% undefined replicates for a required statistic makes its interval acceptance unavailable. This is conditional player-sampling uncertainty over the selected origins: clustering carries repeated player records together but does not estimate uncertainty from shared season shocks or future eras. Development windows may overlap and are dependent; per-origin results describe temporal stability without treating origin count as independent replication. Untouched confirmation remains disjoint in outcome seasons from outcome-exposed development, but no era-level confidence interval or independence of baseball eras is claimed.

Secondary diagnostics: target/category bias where meaningful, top-50 positive-utility capture `sum(max(target,0) in selected group) / sum(max(target,0) in full matched universe)`, with fractional inclusion across score ties at the cutoff; outcome distribution and no-arrival fraction; role, age, cold-start, graduate and coverage slices; rank/score movement by source component; unweighted H3 sensitivity. A zero capture denominator is undefined. Baseline/target errors and failures remain reported. Secondary metrics cannot rescue a primary failure.

For any later U evaluation, primary MAE is in the same declared utility units, not display scores. A proposed default is at least 2% lower equal-origin mean MAE versus each informative baseline and positive paired lower-bound improvement, with the same fold/coverage rules. This is **reserved design only**: there is no admitted U candidate, replacement contract, mapping or executable U evaluation here. Mixed-population and role signed bias must be reported independently of pooled MAE.
