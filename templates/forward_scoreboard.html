How far ahead of the public field our prospect calls land — the median signed lead time, in days, between our call and the field coming to it. We plant our own scoreboard and register it in advance rather than grading ourselves on someone else's.

The bet: the industry eventually comes to our number. A pooled call scores +days when the field moves to us (or a call-up confirms a lead) and −days when the field moves against us before we can retract. Clean self-corrections and calls that aged out with no adverse move are excluded from the pool but stay fully counted in the funnel below — nothing leaves silently.
