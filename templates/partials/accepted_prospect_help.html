How to read surplus and evidence confidence
Y12 surplus
Projected MLB regular-season fantasy production above a fixed replacement reference during the first 12 months: August 26, 2026 through August 25, 2027.
Y24 surplus
The sum across the first 24 months, through August 25, 2028. It adds the second 12-month window to Y12.
Surplus scale
The reference uses standard 5×5 categories: R, HR, RBI, SB and AVG for hitters; W, SV, K, ERA and WHIP for pitchers. It sums standardized category contributions, subtracts a fixed role-specific replacement level and floors each window at zero. Higher is more projected surplus; zero does not mean zero MLB production. These scores are not dollars or a 0–100 rating.
Two-way assets
The better hitter or pitcher branch counts in each window; the two roles are not added together.
Evidence confidence
A description of current and prior statistical input availability, not a probability of success or a prediction interval. High means every role branch has both input slots; moderate means some slots are missing; low means all current/prior slots are missing.

Displayed scores use two decimal places. Full-precision scores determine ranks and remain unchanged in the download. Close displayed ties can therefore have different ranks.
