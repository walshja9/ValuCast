Every prospect we rank meaningfully ahead of the public field, tracked to an outcome — including the calls we backed off ourselves. No call ever leaves this page silently.

The bet being graded: the industry eventually comes to our number. Field moving toward us = win. Field moving away = against us — counting only one direction would make this scorecard impossible to lose. Every status is recomputed from the full archive on every build — nothing is hand-frozen. "Caught up" and "we backed off" describe the latest snapshot; if the gap re-opens later, the call re-opens with it and the headline moves both ways.

Read a row like a position: our number is the thesis, the field's number is the market price, and a call wins when the market comes to our number. Us moving a player up is conviction, not evidence — only the field's movement scores.
