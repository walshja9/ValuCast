Consensus Movement details

The bet being graded: the industry eventually comes to our number. Field moving toward us = win. Field moving away = against us. Every status is recomputed from the full archive on every build — nothing is hand-frozen — and calls can reopen as the gap changes. No call ever leaves this page silently.

Read a row like a position: our number is the thesis, the field's number is the market price, and a call wins when the market comes to our number. Us moving a player up is conviction, not evidence — only the field's movement scores.

Rules, board policy & vintage

Board policy change 2026-07-02: rookie-eligible call-ups now stay ranked instead of leaving the board — 8 already-debuted players entered tracking that week under the same pre-registered rules, disclosed rather than curated. Any call currently on an MLB active roster wears an in the majors chip below; the dynasty-rank bet stays open until he graduates the rookie limits.

Rules pre-registered 2026-07-02 · raw artifact at /aotc-scorecard.json — snapshot it yourself.

Field-consensus vintage: the outside boards we compare against refresh on their own schedules — some fast, some weeks between updates. So the field side of every call can lag reality, and a slow board slows apparent movement in both directions, for us and against us.
